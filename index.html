How It Works

Every message goes through the exact three-stage pipeline from the notebook, running locally in JavaScript.

01

Text Normalization

Converts text to lowercase, strips numeric digits and punctuation, and collapses extra whitespace.

02

TF-IDF Vectorization

Filters English stop words and weights each recognized term by its Inverse Document Frequency (IDF) with L2 normalization.

03

Multinomial Naive Bayes

Combines class log-priors and per-token log-probabilities to compute calibrated Spam vs. Ham confidence scores.

Model & Dataset Details

Trained with TfidfVectorizer(stop_words="english") and MultinomialNB() on a 10,000-row balanced synthetic dataset (5,000 Spam, 5,000 Ham) generated from 20 template phrases.

Note: Because the model was trained on a small synthetic phrase set (94 vocabulary tokens), messages containing words outside that vocabulary rely on the prior distribution.
